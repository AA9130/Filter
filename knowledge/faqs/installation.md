# FAQs — installation

## How long does a water filter installation take?

A typical under-sink RO installation takes about an hour, including pressure testing and flushing the new cartridges before you use the water. A whole-house or villa system takes longer — usually a half day — because it involves cutting into the incoming supply, mounting a frame and isolating the property while the work is done. A softener install is similar, plus the initial regeneration cycle. We flush and test before we leave; water from a brand-new RO membrane should never be drunk straight away.

## Do I need permission from building management to fit a water filter?

For an under-sink filter, normally no — it connects to the existing supply inside your unit and nothing structural changes. For anything that touches the building's incoming supply, riser or common areas, yes, and you should get it in writing before work is scheduled. Villa work on your own incoming line is your decision, though community developments sometimes have rules about external equipment. If you are unsure which category your job falls into, tell us the building and we will tell you which approval, if any, applies.
