# FAQs — pricing

## How much does RO installation cost in Dubai?

We do not publish a price, because the number would be wrong for most people who read it. What you pay depends on the system your water justifies, whether a booster pump is needed for your building's pressure, whether a drinking faucet can use an existing tap hole or needs one drilled, and how far the unit sits from the drain and the feed point. What we do instead: a technician visits free of charge, measures your water, and gives you a fixed written quotation in AED before anything is bought or fitted. There is no call-out fee and no obligation.
