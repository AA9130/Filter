# FAQs — service

## Can you service a system another company installed?

Yes — servicing and repairing systems fitted by someone else is a large part of what we do, across all major brands, and we stock the common consumables for them. Two honest caveats. Some imported units use proprietary cartridge formats that have to be ordered rather than carried on the van, which affects timing. And if a system is beyond economical repair, we will say so rather than keep charging you for repairs on it.

## Which Emirates do you cover?

All seven: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain. Teams are based in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, with scheduled routes covering the northern Emirates. Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah are usually same-day; Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and Umm Al Quwain are typically within 24 hours; Fujairah is typically within 48 hours. Send us your location and we will confirm the next slot for your specific area rather than for the emirate in general.

## Do you offer emergency repairs at night or on Fridays?

Yes — the emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. Leaks and a total loss of flow are treated as priority call-outs, because both get worse while they wait. If you have a leak right now, close the system's feed valve before you call: it stops the damage while we are on the way.

## Is the water test and demonstration really free?

Yes, and with no obligation. A technician tests your water in front of you, explains what the reading means, and leaves you a written quotation. There is no call-out fee whether or not you buy anything. The reason we can offer it is straightforward commercially: most people who see their own TDS and hardness reading make a better-informed decision, and a well-matched system generates fewer complaints than an oversold one.

## Can you move my water filter system to a new property?

Yes. A relocation is an uninstall, a transport and a reinstall, and it is worth treating as one job rather than three: the system should be drained and sanitised before it travels, the cartridges are usually due for replacement anyway once a unit has been opened, and the new property's pressure and drain arrangement may not match the old one. We check all of that at the new address rather than assuming the old configuration still fits.

## Do you install water filtration for restaurants, offices and labour accommodation?

Yes — commercial work is a distinct part of the business, because the requirements are different. Duty cycles are far higher, so cartridge and membrane sizing that suits a family will fail in a cafeteria. Kitchens usually need scale reduction ahead of coffee and steam equipment, not just drinking-water treatment. And commercial premises are inspected, so water-quality records matter as much as the equipment. We size to measured demand and keep service records for the site.
