# FAQs — water quality

## Is UAE tap water safe to drink without a filter?

Mains water in the UAE is produced mainly by seawater desalination and leaves the plant treated to a drinking-water standard. What a filter addresses is the journey after that: the water travels through distribution pipework and, in most UAE buildings, sits in a ground or rooftop storage tank before it reaches your tap. That stage can reintroduce sediment, rust from older pipework, a chlorine taste, and — if a tank is poorly maintained — bacteria. A point-of-use filter is what fixes the quality at the moment you drink, rather than at the moment the water was produced.

## What causes limescale on my taps and glass?

Dissolved calcium and magnesium — water hardness. When hard water evaporates or is heated, those minerals come out of solution and stay behind as the white, chalky deposit you see on shower glass, taps and inside a kettle. It is not dirt and it is not a sign the water is unsafe; it is a sign of what the water is carrying. Nothing you fit at the kitchen sink will change it, because the scale is forming everywhere the water goes. Reducing hardness at the point of entry, with a softener, is the only treatment that stops it forming rather than cleaning it up afterwards.

## How do I reduce the TDS of my drinking water?

Reverse osmosis is the practical answer for a home. A membrane rejects most of the dissolved content — typically 95 to 99% of total dissolved solids — which no cartridge filter can do, because sediment and carbon stages target particles, chlorine, taste and odour rather than dissolved salts. Distillation also works but is impractical at household scale. Worth knowing: very low TDS water tastes flat to most people, which is why a remineralising or alkaline post-stage is common on UAE installations. TDS is also a blunt instrument — it tells you how much is dissolved, not what.

## My villa is supplied by water tanker. Does that change what I need?

Yes, in two ways. Tanker and tank-fed supply carries more sediment — sand and rust picked up in handling and storage — so a point-of-entry sediment stage stops that reaching everything downstream, and a self-cleaning pre-filter avoids the cartridge blocking every few weeks. And because the water has been stored rather than delivered straight from the network, UV disinfection on the drinking-water line addresses the risk that storage introduces. Filtration alone does not do that: a sediment or carbon cartridge is not a disinfection stage.
