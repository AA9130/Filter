# Water filtration in Abu Dhabi

AquaPure covers Abu Dhabi from a base in the emirate, serving island apartments, the villa suburbs around Khalifa City and Mohammed Bin Zayed City, and the commercial and industrial premises in Mussafah. The mix of villa stock and heavy commercial demand makes Abu Dhabi the emirate where we fit the widest range of system sizes.

**Typical response**: Same-day. Usually same-day on the island and in the main suburbs; outlying areas are scheduled.

**Water supplied by**: Abu Dhabi Distribution Company (ADDC)

## Property types

Abu Dhabi is more villa-heavy than Dubai once you leave the island. Khalifa City, Al Reef, Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Shakhbout City and Al Shamkha are largely villa and townhouse communities with their own tanks and pump rooms — the configuration point-of-entry filtration is designed for. On the island and Al Reem, apartment towers dominate and the practical answer is a point-of-use system. Mussafah is different again: workshops, cafeterias, labour accommodation and light industry, where duty cycles are high enough that domestic sizing fails.

## Water supply

Water is distributed by ADDC and produced predominantly by desalination, transmitted across the emirate before it reaches a property.

Distances in Abu Dhabi are longer than in Dubai, and a larger share of the housing stock is villa stock with individual ground or roof storage. Where water is stored on the property rather than drawn continuously from the network, sediment settling and summer tank temperature both become part of the picture.

Groundwater in the emirate is used extensively for irrigation and landscaping. It is not the potable supply, but it is worth knowing about if a property has a separate irrigation line — a filter fitted on the wrong line is a mistake we occasionally get called to correct.

## Common requests

### Villa point-of-entry systems

Sediment, carbon and — where hardness is the complaint — a softener, sized to the villa's actual demand and mounted in the pump room or on the roof rather than wherever there happens to be a wall.

### Commercial RO for Mussafah premises

Cafeterias, workshops and accommodation blocks, sized to measured demand. Commercial jobs come with service records, because commercial premises are inspected and the paperwork matters as much as the plant.

### Scale reduction ahead of kitchen equipment

Coffee machines, combi ovens and steamers fail on scale long before anything else in a commercial kitchen does. Treating the feed to that equipment is often more urgent than treating the drinking-water tap.

### Scheduled AMC visits

Because travel across the emirate is a real cost, Abu Dhabi customers get more value out of scheduled maintenance than out of calling per visit.

## Local considerations

### Pump-room installations, not cupboard installations

A villa point-of-entry system belongs in the pump room or on the roof, on a frame, with isolation valves and a drain port so a service visit does not mean shutting off the whole villa. Retrofitting valves later costs more than fitting them at the start.

### Commercial duty cycles change the sizing

A cartridge rated for a family's daily draw is exhausted in weeks in a cafeteria. We size commercial systems on measured demand and set service intervals from throughput rather than the calendar.

### Travel windows for outlying areas

Al Shamkha, Al Samha and the further suburbs are covered on scheduled routes rather than ad hoc. Booking a visit into an existing route is faster than requesting an emergency slot for a non-urgent job.
