# Water filtration in Ajman

AquaPure covers Ajman on scheduled routes from our Sharjah and Dubai bases, typically within 24 hours. The work here spans two quite different property types: high-rise apartment towers in Emirates City and Al Nuaimiya, and villa plots in Al Mowaihat, Al Rumailah and towards Al Zorah.

## Property types

Emirates City and the Corniche towers put a lot of Ajman's population in high-rise apartments served by building storage tanks and booster pumps — point-of-use territory. The villa districts inland are the opposite case: your own incoming line, your own tank, and the option of treating the whole property. Ajman's industrial area adds commercial demand at a smaller scale than Mussafah but with the same sizing logic.

## Water supply

Ajman is supplied by Etihad Water and Electricity, the federal utility that took over the former FEWA network serving the northern Emirates. Supply is predominantly desalinated.

Building storage is the dominant local factor, as it is across the northern Emirates. In towers, water is held in tanks and lifted; in villas, it is held in a ground or roof tank. Both mean the water you drink has been standing somewhere before it reached the tap.

Parts of some developments take tanker top-up. Where they do, sediment loading is higher and disinfection on the drinking-water line is the appropriate response, because a sediment or carbon cartridge does not disinfect anything.

## Common requests

### Under-sink RO in tower apartments

Emirates City and Al Nuaimiya apartments, where pressure at the tap and cupboard space are the two constraints worth checking before choosing a system.

### Villa pre-filtration and softening

A self-cleaning sediment pre-filter first — it rinses out through a valve instead of blocking a cartridge — then carbon, and a softener where scale is the complaint.

### Scheduled cartridge replacement

Because Ajman runs on scheduled routes, booking a filter change into a planned visit is both faster and easier to arrange than calling on the day.

### Repairs and second-hand system revivals

Systems inherited with a property, often several years past a service. Whether reviving one is worth it depends on the membrane, and we will say if it is not.

## Local considerations

### Scheduled coverage rewards planning

Ajman is served on routes, not from a permanent local base. Routine work booked in advance lands sooner than routine work requested as urgent, and emergencies — leaks, total loss of flow — are slotted in ahead of routine visits.

### High towers, pressure first

The same rule as Dubai Marina applies in Emirates City: check static pressure at the tap before choosing a system, because an RO membrane starved of pressure produces slowly and fails early.

### Villa tanks are part of the system

Treating villa water while ignoring the tank it sits in solves half the problem. Tank condition is worth inspecting at the same visit as the filtration, even though cleaning it is a separate job.
