AquaPure installs, services and repairs water filtration systems across Dubai — from single under-sink RO units in Marina and JVC apartments to whole-villa systems in Mirdif, Al Barsha and the Ranches. ## Property types

Dubai's housing splits sharply for filtration purposes. High-rise apartments make up most of the stock in Marina, JLT, Business Bay, Downtown, JVC and Silicon Oasis; there you treat one outlet, because the incoming supply and the building's storage are not yours to touch. Villa and townhouse communities — Mirdif, Al Barsha, Jumeirah, Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills — have their own incoming line, tanks and pump arrangement, which puts point-of-entry treatment on the table.

## Water supply

Mains water in Dubai is supplied by DEWA and is produced predominantly by seawater desalination. It leaves the plant treated to a drinking-water standard.

What changes it before you drink it is the building. Most Dubai towers hold water in ground or roof tanks and lift it with booster pumps, and most villas have tanks of their own. Storage is where sediment settles, where temperature rises through the summer, and where a poorly maintained tank can introduce a problem that no amount of upstream treatment prevents.

The older districts — parts of Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Quoz — have the oldest pipework in the emirate, and that is where we most often find sediment and rust staining rather than a dissolved-solids complaint.

## Common requests

### Under-sink RO in an apartment

The default Dubai job. A compact or tankless RO inside the sink cupboard, a dedicated drinking faucet, and a booster pump where the tower's pressure at your floor needs one — common on higher floors and in older buildings.

### Whole-villa filtration and softening

A sediment stage at the point of entry, carbon for chlorine, taste and odour, and a softener where limescale on glass and fittings is the actual complaint. Configured after a water test rather than sold as a package.

### Filter and membrane replacement

Cartridge changes on systems we fitted and on systems fitted by someone else, including brands whose cartridges have to be ordered rather than carried on the van.

### Emergency leak call-outs

Under-sink leaks, split housings and failed tank fittings. These are treated as priority because the cabinet damage compounds while the job waits.

## Local considerations

### Building approval on anything past your own unit

An under-sink filter inside your apartment normally needs no permission. Anything that touches the riser, the building's incoming supply or a common area does, and it should be in writing before the job is scheduled. Tell us the building and we will tell you which category the work falls into.

### Pressure on high floors

Weak pressure at the tap starves an RO membrane: the system produces slowly, sends more water to the drain and wears out early. On upper floors we check static pressure before quoting, because fitting a system without the pump it needs creates a complaint six months later.

### Cupboard depth in newer towers

Kitchen cabinets in a lot of recent Dubai apartments will not take a conventional RO plus a separate storage tank. That is the situation a tankless unit exists for, and it is worth measuring the cupboard before choosing.
