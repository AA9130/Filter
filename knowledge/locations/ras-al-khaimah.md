# Water filtration in Ras Al Khaimah

AquaPure covers Ras Al Khaimah on scheduled routes, typically within 24 hours. RAK's mix — coastal villa and townhouse communities like Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab, city apartments around Al Nakheel and Al Qusaidat, and spread-out inland districts towards the mountains — means the same emirate contains both the simplest and the most involved jobs we do.

## Property types

The master-planned coastal communities are the clearest case for point-of-entry treatment: villas and townhouses on their own supply, with tanks and pump arrangements that are consistent across a development, so a configuration proven on one property usually transfers. City apartments around Al Nakheel and Al Qusaidat are point-of-use jobs. The inland and northern districts — Al Rams, towards the mountains — are more spread out, and travel is a genuine part of scheduling there.

## Water supply

Ras Al Khaimah is supplied by Etihad Water and Electricity. As across the UAE, the mains supply is predominantly desalinated.

Two local factors matter more here than in Dubai. Coastal communities are exposed to salt-laden air, which shortens the life of external metal fittings and frames — mounting a point-of-entry system where it is sheltered is not cosmetic. And the inland and northern districts are the parts of the country where storage tanks and tanker top-up are most common, which shifts the recommendation towards sediment removal and disinfection.

We do not publish a water-quality figure for RAK or any other emirate. Real values vary by supply zone, season and the property's own storage, and the only number worth acting on is the one measured at your tap.

## Common requests

### Whole-villa systems in coastal communities

Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab townhouses and villas: sediment, carbon and softening at the point of entry, plus an under-sink RO for drinking water.

### Sediment control on tank-fed properties

A self-cleaning pre-filter is the right first fitting where sand and rust are the daily reality, because a disposable cartridge in that duty blocks faster than anyone wants to keep replacing it.

### UV on the drinking line

Where a property is fed from a tank or a tanker rather than continuously from the network, disinfection addresses what storage introduces. Filtration on its own does not.

### Annual maintenance for second homes

A number of RAK properties are used seasonally. A system left standing for months needs sanitising and flushing before use, and that is worth scheduling rather than discovering on arrival.

## Local considerations

### Mount equipment out of the salt air

In the coastal communities, a frame or housing mounted in the open weathers noticeably faster. Sheltered mounting, and stainless fixings where they are exposed, is the difference between a system that looks serviceable in five years and one that does not.

### Standing water in seasonally used homes

Water that has sat in a system for months is not the same as water that has sat for a weekend. Sanitisation and a full flush before use — and, ideally, isolating the system before a long absence — is the honest procedure.

### Travel windows for the inland districts

Al Rams and the inland areas are covered on the same routes as the city, but slotting a routine visit into a planned run is much faster than requesting one outside it.
