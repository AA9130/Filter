# Water filtration in Sharjah

AquaPure operates in Sharjah from a base in the emirate, and the work here is dominated by apartments — Al Nahda, Al Majaz, Al Taawun, Abu Shagara and Muweilah are dense family-apartment districts where a well-fitted under-sink system is the whole job. Sharjah is also where we do the most cartridge-replacement work per household, because family apartments draw hard on drinking-water systems.

**Typical response**: Same-day. Usually same-day across the city; outlying areas within 24 hours.

**Water supplied by**: Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority (SEWA)

## Property types

Sharjah's stock is more apartment-weighted than Dubai's or Abu Dhabi's, and the apartments are more often occupied by larger families. That combination matters: a family of six draws through a drinking-water system several times faster than a couple does, which shortens cartridge intervals and makes storage capacity — or a tankless system's continuous output — a real consideration rather than a specification detail. Villa areas exist, particularly towards Al Mamzar and the outer districts, and the industrial area brings commercial work.

## Water supply

Water in Sharjah is supplied by SEWA, the emirate's own authority — distinct from DEWA in Dubai and from Etihad Water and Electricity in the northern Emirates. Supply is predominantly desalinated.

The practical difference in Sharjah is building storage. Apartment blocks here almost always hold water in tanks and lift it by pump, and in older blocks the tanks and risers are older too. Sediment and taste complaints are more common than dissolved-solids complaints in the districts with the oldest stock.

Some outlying areas rely on tanker delivery for part of their supply, which changes the recommendation: stored and handled water is the case for disinfection on the drinking line, not just filtration.

## Common requests

### Under-sink RO for family apartments

The most common Sharjah installation, and the one where storage capacity matters most. A larger household drawing several litres at once is the case for a tanked system rather than a slim tankless one.

### Cartridge replacement on a shorter cycle

Higher household draw and older building storage both shorten pre-filter life. We set the interval from your usage and what the old cartridges actually look like when we pull them, not from a generic schedule.

### Repairs on systems bought elsewhere

A large share of Sharjah call-outs are for units bought from a retailer and never serviced. Leaking housings, exhausted carbon and flat storage tanks account for most of them.

### Commercial systems in the industrial area

Workshops, staff accommodation and canteens, sized on measured demand.

## Local considerations

### Household size changes the system, not just the schedule

This is the single most useful local point in Sharjah. A drinking-water system that suits two people is undersized for six, and the symptom is not a fault — it is slow refill and a family that stops using the filtered tap. Sizing for actual occupancy is worth more than any extra filtration stage.

### Older blocks, sediment first

In the districts with the oldest buildings, a sediment stage does more for the water than an extra polishing stage does. It also protects the carbon and the membrane behind it, which is what makes the cartridges last.

### Landlord permission for anything structural

Most Sharjah installations are in rented apartments. An under-sink system connects to existing plumbing and is normally straightforward, but drilling a granite worktop for a drinking faucet is worth clearing with the landlord first. We can usually fit the faucet without new drilling.
