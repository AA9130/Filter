# Water filtration in Umm Al Quwain

AquaPure covers Umm Al Quwain on scheduled northern routes, typically within 24 hours. UAQ is the smallest emirate we serve and almost entirely low-rise, which makes the work unusually consistent: most properties have their own incoming line and their own tank, so most jobs are point-of-entry plus a drinking-water system.

## Property types

Villas, townhouses and small apartment blocks, concentrated along the coast at Al Salamah, Al Raas, Al Humrah and the Marina, with Falaj Al Mualla inland. Very little high-rise stock means very few of the constraints that dominate Dubai apartment work — building approval, riser access, cupboard depth — and correspondingly more scope to treat the whole property properly.

## Water supply

Umm Al Quwain is supplied by Etihad Water and Electricity, and the mains supply is predominantly desalinated.

As across the northern Emirates, property-level storage is the dominant local factor: water is held in a ground or roof tank before use. Coastal properties also share Ras Al Khaimah's exposure to salt-laden air, which is worth accounting for when siting external equipment.

Inland at Falaj Al Mualla, properties are further apart and tanker delivery features more, which shifts the specification towards robust sediment removal and disinfection rather than additional polishing stages.

## Common requests

### Whole-property filtration for villas

The standard UAQ job: a sediment stage, carbon, and softening where hardness is the complaint — sized to the villa and mounted where it can be serviced.

### Under-sink RO for drinking water

Fitted alongside the point-of-entry system rather than instead of it, because the two treat different problems.

### Disinfection on tank-fed supply

UV, or a chemical-free contact media where a power supply at the point of installation is inconvenient.

### Scheduled maintenance

Routine visits grouped into northern routes, which is both quicker to arrange and less disruptive than ad hoc call-outs.

## Local considerations

### Low-rise stock means fewer constraints

In UAQ you can usually do the job properly the first time: point-of-entry treatment is available to most properties, there is space to mount a serviceable frame, and no building management approval is in the way. It is worth taking that opportunity rather than fitting only a kitchen filter.

### Coastal siting for external equipment

Salt air near the coast shortens the life of exposed metalwork. Sheltered mounting and corrosion-resistant fixings are a specification decision, not a finish.

### Inland properties: robustness over refinement

At Falaj Al Mualla and the inland plots, the priorities are a sediment stage that can be rinsed rather than replaced, and disinfection. Extra polishing stages add cost without addressing what is actually in stored, tankered water.
