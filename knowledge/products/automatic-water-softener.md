# AquaPure Automatic Water Softener

An automatic ion-exchange softener that reduces the dissolved calcium and magnesium behind limescale, sized to your measured hardness and actual household demand. It regenerates itself with salt on a timer or on volume treated, and it treats the whole property rather than one tap.

## Key facts

- Reduces hardness across every outlet, so scale stops forming
- Automatic regeneration — timer or metered, set at commissioning
- Salt is flushed to drain during regeneration; it does not make water taste salty
- Not a drinking-water treatment — dissolved solids other than hardness pass through

## Features

- Automatic regeneration — it manages itself once we set it up
- Resin volume sized to your household's actual usage
- Brine tank with a safety float against overflow
- Salt supply and refill scheduling if you would rather not carry bags
- Noticeable difference to skin, hair and laundry within days
- Protects geysers, washing machines, taps and glassware from scale

## Who it suits

### Villas with scale on fittings and glass

The clearest case, and where the return is largest because the same water reaches several bathrooms, a geyser and a washing machine.

### Households losing appliances early

Scale on a heating element is both a failure risk and a running-cost increase.

### Commercial kitchens and laundries

Coffee, steam and dishwashing equipment specify soft feed water, and scale is the leading cause of failure in it.

## How it is fitted

Installed into the incoming supply with a bypass and a drain connection for regeneration, and — where an irrigation line shares the supply — with a take-off ahead of the softener, since plants gain nothing from softened water.

The salt tank needs to be somewhere you can actually carry salt to. Solving that at installation rather than afterwards is worth the thought.

## Maintenance

### Salt level

A softener out of salt keeps passing water untreated with no obvious symptom until the scale returns.

### Brine draw and drain

Checked at service. A failed brine valve is the fault behind almost every genuine complaint of salty water.

### Settings versus reality

Household usage changes. Regeneration settings should be revisited when it does, rather than left as commissioned.

## Considerations

### Metered regeneration where budget allows

It regenerates on volume treated rather than on a schedule, which uses less salt and less water.

### Protect the resin

A sediment and carbon stage ahead of the softener materially extends resin life, particularly on chlorinated supply.

### Size on hardness and demand together

Specifying from household size alone, without a hardness figure, is specifying from half the information.
