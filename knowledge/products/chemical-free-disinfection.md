# AquaPure Chemical-Free Disinfection Unit

A contact-disinfection stage using a ceramic media that acts on bacteria as water passes through it — no chemicals dosed, no electricity, and no lamp to replace. It is the alternative to UV where mains power at the installation point is inconvenient or where a power cut must not interrupt protection.

## Key facts

- Acts on contact — nothing is dosed into the water
- Needs no electricity, so a power cut does not interrupt it
- No lamp or sleeve to replace, unlike a UV chamber
- Leaves taste, odour and mineral content unchanged

## Features

- Kills bacteria on contact — nothing is dosed into your water
- Needs no electricity, so it keeps working through a power cut
- No lamp to replace, unlike a UV chamber
- Leaves taste, odour and mineral content unchanged
- Fits at the point of entry, or in-line before a drinking tap
- Silent, with no moving parts to fail

## Who it suits

### Properties where power at the fitting point is awkward

The main reason to choose this over UV. No supply to run, no circuit to protect.

### Seasonally used and remote properties

Nothing to fail silently while nobody is there, and no lamp whose life is quietly running out.

### Anyone wanting disinfection without chemicals

No chlorine dosing, no by-products, no change to how the water tastes.

## How it is fitted

Fitted at the point of entry, or in-line ahead of a drinking tap, after filtration — the same rule as UV: particles and turbidity reduce the effectiveness of any contact or light-based disinfection stage.

Silent, with no moving parts, which makes siting easier than a powered chamber.

## Maintenance

### Media has a service life

Longer than a UV lamp's, but not indefinite. It is checked and replaced on schedule.

### Upstream filtration still matters

Sediment reaching the media reduces contact effectiveness and shortens its life.

## Considerations

### It removes nothing

Like UV, it is a disinfection stage rather than a filtration stage. Dissolved solids, chlorine and sediment all need their own treatment.

### Compare honestly with UV

UV is well understood, standardised and easy to monitor with an alarm. Contact media wins on power independence and on having nothing that fails weekly. Which matters more depends on the property.
