# AquaPure Compact Under-Sink RO

An all-in-one under-sink reverse osmosis system with the storage tank built into the body, so it occupies a single footprint inside a normal apartment sink cupboard instead of needing a separate tank beside it. It suits households that draw several litres at a time and have cupboard width but not room for two objects.

## Key facts

- Tank integrated into the unit — one footprint, not two
- Stored purified water, so a fast flow at the faucet from a modest pump
- Quick-connect cartridges, changed in minutes on a service visit
- Built-in leak detection and a pressure-reducing valve

## Features

- Storage tank built into the unit — no separate tank to find room for
- Quick-connect cartridges we can swap in minutes on a service visit
- Built-in leak detector cuts the supply before water reaches your floor
- Pressure-reducing valve protects the membrane and your plumbing
- Dedicated lead-free drinking faucet fitted at your sink
- Booster pump option for buildings with weak mains pressure

## Who it suits

### Apartment kitchens with cupboard width

The common case. If the cupboard can take one unit but not a unit plus a tank, this is the system designed for that situation.

### Families drawing several litres at once

Stored water is what gives a fast flow when filling a jug or a pot. A tankless unit purifies on demand and delivers more slowly.

### Buildings with adequate mains pressure

Where pressure at your floor is weak, the booster pump option becomes part of the specification rather than an extra.

## How it is fitted

Mounted inside the sink cupboard, plumbed from the cold feed with an isolation valve, and drained to the sink waste through a properly clamped saddle.

A dedicated lead-free drinking faucet is fitted at the sink — using an existing tap hole or sink knockout where one is available, which matters in rented apartments and on stone worktops.

## Maintenance

### Pre-filters about every 6 months

Quick-connect formats make this a short visit rather than a plumbing job.

### Membrane every 2 to 3 years

Judged on the treated TDS reading rather than the date.

### Tank air charge

Checked at service. A tank that has lost its charge presents as low pressure at the faucet.

## Considerations

### Measure the cupboard, including depth

Behind the waste trap is where the space runs out. This is what decides between this unit and the tankless one more often than any specification.

### Confirm pressure before buying

A membrane starved of feed pressure produces slowly, drains more and fails early — and the symptom looks like a faulty system.
