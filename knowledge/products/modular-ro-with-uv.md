# AquaPure Modular RO with UV Option

A serviceable modular under-sink RO on a steel frame with an optional UV stage. It is what we fit for villas on tanker or roof-tank supply, where drinking water needs disinfecting as well as purifying — because filtration and disinfection are different jobs and a cartridge does not do the second one.

## Key facts

- Modular housings — each stage inspected and changed independently
- Optional UV stage for tank-fed and tanker-fed properties
- Pressure gauge, so a technician can read the membrane's condition
- Optional booster pump where feed pressure is low

## Features

- Modular housings — each stage inspected and changed independently

- Optional booster pump where mains pressure is low
- Antimicrobial cartridge option to resist biofilm inside the housings
- Pressure gauge so our technician can read the membrane's condition
- Floor-standing or wall-mounted frame to suit the cupboard

## Who it suits

### Villas on tank or tanker supply

The primary case. Stored water carries a risk that filtration does not address, and the UV stage is the part that does.

### Properties used seasonally

Where a system may stand unused for months, serviceability and disinfection both matter more than compactness.

### Anyone who wants the system readable

A gauge and independent housings mean the system's condition can be assessed rather than guessed at.

## How it is fitted

Floor-standing or wall-mounted on a steel frame, sized to the cupboard or the utility space, with isolation valves and clearance to open each housing.

The UV stage sits after filtration, never before it: particles shield organisms from the light, so a UV chamber fed unfiltered water delivers much less protection than its rating suggests.

## Maintenance

### Stages changed independently

You replace what is exhausted rather than a sealed cartridge block.

### UV lamp annually, on running hours

Output falls long before a lamp stops lighting. The quartz sleeve is cleaned or replaced at the same visit.

### Gauge readings recorded

A growing pressure differential reports a developing blockage before flow noticeably drops.

## Considerations

### Larger footprint than a sealed unit

Modularity costs space. In an apartment cupboard that is usually the wrong trade; in a villa utility space it is the right one.

### UV needs power that stays on

A UV chamber with no power is a plain pipe. Worth siting on a circuit nobody switches off.
