# AquaPure Self-Cleaning Villa Pre-Filter

A point-of-entry sediment filter with a reusable mesh element that you rinse by opening a drain valve, instead of a cartridge you replace. It is the first fitting we recommend on a villa fed by tanker or a storage tank, because sand and rust in that duty block a disposable cartridge faster than anyone wants to keep replacing it.

## Key facts

- Reusable mesh — rinsed clean through a valve, not replaced
- Fitted at the point of entry, before everything else in the property
- Clear bowl so the sand and rust it is catching is visible
- Available for cold lines and for hot water lines

## Features

- Self-cleaning: open the drain valve and the mesh flushes itself
- Reusable mesh element — no cartridge to keep buying
- Clear bowl, so you can actually see the sand and rust it is catching
- Protects every filter, softener, geyser and appliance downstream
- Versions for cold lines and for hot water lines
- Pressure-gauge version so blockage is visible before flow drops
- Fitted at the point of entry, before anything else in the property

## Who it suits

### Villas on tanker or tank supply

Where sediment loading is high enough that a disposable cartridge becomes a recurring chore.

### Properties with older incoming pipework

Rust and scale flaking from old galvanised pipe is exactly what this stage catches.

### Anyone with a softener or filtration downstream

Sediment in a resin bed or on a membrane is difficult to recover from. This is the stage that prevents it.

## How it is fitted

Fitted into the incoming supply ahead of every other treatment stage, with the drain valve piped to a point where flushing it is convenient rather than messy.

A pressure-gauge version makes developing blockage visible before flow to the property drops, which is worth having where nobody is checking the bowl regularly.

## Maintenance

### Rinse rather than replace

Open the drain valve and the mesh flushes itself. Frequency depends entirely on loading — weekly on a heavily tankered property, far less on mains supply.

### Inspect the mesh periodically

A reusable element is not a permanent one. It is checked at service visits.

### Watch the pressure differential

The honest indicator that a rinse is due, ahead of any visible symptom.

## Considerations

### It is a sediment stage, nothing more

It does not remove chlorine, taste, hardness or dissolved solids, and it does not disinfect. Its value is protecting what comes after it.

### Needs a drain point

The flush has to go somewhere. Solving that at installation is easier than living with a bucket.
