# AquaPure Slim Tankless RO

A tankless reverse osmosis system that purifies water as you draw it rather than storing it, in a slim upright casing that fits cupboards a conventional system will not. It is what we recommend when cupboard depth is genuinely the constraint, and it needs a higher-output membrane and pump to deliver a usable flow.

## Key facts

- Nothing is stored — water is purified on demand
- Slim upright casing for narrow or shallow under-sink cupboards
- TDS readout on the unit, so you can check the water yourself
- Filter-change reminder plus low-pressure and leak warnings

## Features

- Tankless — water is purified as you draw it, nothing sits in storage
- Slim upright casing fits narrow under-sink cupboards
- TDS readout on the unit so you can check your water any time
- Filter-change reminder, plus low-pressure and leak warning lights
- Neat enough to stand on a countertop where there is no cupboard
- Cartridges designed for tool-free replacement

## Who it suits

### Tight apartment cupboards

The reason this system exists. No storage vessel means far less depth and no second object to find room for.

### Households that dislike stored water

On-demand purification means nothing sits between draws.

### Countertop installations

Neat enough to stand on a worktop where there is no usable cupboard at all.

## How it is fitted

Stands upright inside the cupboard or on the counter, plumbed from the cold feed with an isolation valve and drained to the sink waste.

Because production is on demand, the pump and membrane are doing more work per litre than in a tanked system — which is why feed pressure and a stable power supply both matter here.

## Maintenance

### Tool-free cartridge changes

Designed so a service visit does not mean dismantling the cupboard.

### Watch the on-unit TDS readout

The most useful early warning of a membrane losing rejection that any home system offers.

### Membrane every 2 to 3 years

Higher-output membranes are not longer-lived; the interval is the same.

## Considerations

### Flow rate versus household size

A large family filling several containers at once will find on-demand production slower than a tank. This is the real trade, and it is worth being honest about before purchase.

### Higher purchase price for the same treatment

You are paying for the space saving and the on-demand behaviour, not for better filtration.
