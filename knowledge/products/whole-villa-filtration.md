# AquaPure Whole-Villa Filtration System

Large-capacity housings on a steel frame that treat every outlet in the property, configured as single, double or triple stage after a water test. More stages are not automatically better — stages your water does not need cost money at installation and again at every service.

## Key facts

- Treats every tap, shower and appliance in the property
- Single, double or triple stage, configured to your water test
- Large housings for longer cartridge life and strong whole-property flow
- Gauge and drain ports so servicing does not mean draining the villa

## Features

- Single, double or triple stage, configured to your water test
- Large housings for long cartridge life and strong flow to the whole villa
- Clear or solid bowls — clear lets you see when a change is due
- Steel frame for wall or floor mounting in the pump room or roof
- Gauge and drain ports so servicing does not mean draining the villa
- Removes sediment, chlorine, taste and odour before the first tap

## Who it suits

### Villas and townhouses

You control the incoming line and there is space for a serviceable frame — the two conditions point-of-entry treatment needs.

### Properties with shower or appliance complaints

Chlorine smell in the shower, sediment in the washing machine, rust marks in a bath. None of those have a point-of-use solution.

### Buildings and accommodation blocks

One treatment point serving many outlets, sized on measured demand.

## How it is fitted

Mounted on a steel frame in the pump room, plant room or on the roof, cut into the incoming supply with isolation and bypass valves so the property keeps water during a service.

Housing size is chosen for cartridge life and for flow to the furthest outlet. Undersizing to save space starves the property's flow and shortens cartridge life at the same time.

## Maintenance

### Cartridge intervals follow loading

Set from what the removed cartridges actually look like on this property, not from a generic schedule.

### Pressure drop is the health indicator

A gauge before and after reports a developing blockage before flow noticeably falls.

### Clear bowls make it visible

Where the system is not in direct sunlight, a clear bowl lets you see when a change is due.

## Considerations

### It is not a drinking-water system

Point-of-entry filtration does not remove dissolved solids and does not disinfect. A drinking-water stage at the kitchen sink is a separate fitting, usually fitted alongside.

### Hardness needs a softener, not a filter

If the complaint is white scale, the answer is ion exchange. Filtration stages will not change it.

### Site it sheltered, especially near the coast

Salt-laden air shortens the life of exposed metalwork noticeably.
