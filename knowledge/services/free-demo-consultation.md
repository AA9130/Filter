## What it involves

The water test is the step that turns a purchase into a decision. TDS tells you how much is dissolved in your water; hardness tells you how much of that is the calcium and magnesium behind limescale. Those two readings, taken at your tap, point to different technologies — and they differ between buildings, between risers in the same building, and before and after a tank has been cleaned.

That is why we do not publish a water-quality figure for any emirate, and why any recommendation made without a reading is a guess dressed as advice. The consultation is the reading plus the explanation: what it means for your property, what it does not mean, and which of the available options the number actually justifies.

## Common problems

### A recommendation made without a reading

- **Cause**: Selling a package rather than diagnosing a property.
- **Fix**: Ask for the measured figures and where they were taken. Any specification should be traceable to a reading.

### TDS treated as a quality score

- **Cause**: It is the number most easily measured, so it gets over-interpreted.
- **Fix**: Understand it as a quantity, not an identity. A low TDS reading does not by itself mean the water is safe, and a high one does not by itself mean it is unsafe.

## Choosing

### Test before you shop, not after

The reading narrows the options considerably, which makes every subsequent conversation shorter.

### Test at the outlet you use

A reading from a garden tap or a bathroom tap can differ from the kitchen cold tap you drink from.

### Ask what the reading does not cover

TDS and hardness do not identify specific contaminants. If you have a specific concern, laboratory analysis is the honest route, and we will say so rather than pretend a handheld meter answers it.

## UAE context

An on-site test matters more in the UAE than a national or emirate-level figure ever could, because the biggest variable is not the utility's output — it is what happens between the network and your tap. Building storage, riser age, tanker delivery and how long water has been standing all move the reading.

It is also why we decline to publish a TDS figure for Dubai, Abu Dhabi or anywhere else. Two apartments in the same tower can read differently. The number that should decide what you buy is the one measured where you drink.
