# Water Filter AMC Plans Across the UAE

An AMC is a fixed annual fee covering scheduled service visits, the consumables listed in your tier, sanitisation, leak testing, labour and call-out. AquaPure contacts you when a service is due rather than waiting for you to notice a problem. Contracts are available for apartments, villas and commercial premises, differing in visit count and what consumables are included, and are transferable if you move within the UAE.

## Key facts

- Fixed annual fee covering scheduled visits, listed consumables, labour and call-out
- We initiate the visit — the common failure is forgetting, not choosing to skip
- Tiers differ by visit count and included consumables, not by quality of work
- Commercial tiers include water-quality records for inspected premises

## What it involves

An AMC is a scheduling and accountability arrangement as much as a commercial one. What you buy is a defined number of visits per year, a defined list of consumables included, and the labour and call-out to deliver them — plus the obligation on us to make contact when a visit is due.

The reason that last part carries most of the value is behavioural. Almost nobody decides to skip a filter change. What happens is that six months pass, the water still comes out of the tap, and nothing prompts anyone. Meanwhile an exhausted carbon cartridge is passing chlorine onto a membrane that costs many times more than the cartridge did.

## Who needs it

### Households that would rather not track it

The honest majority. If you cannot say when your filters were last changed, a contract is aimed exactly at you.

### Villas with several treatment points

A point-of-entry system, a softener and a drinking-water system have three different schedules. Consolidating them into planned visits is simpler and cheaper than three separate call-outs.

### Commercial premises

Higher duty cycles mean shorter intervals, and inspected premises need the records as much as the servicing.

### Properties outside Dubai and Abu Dhabi

Where attendance is on scheduled routes, having your visits inside a planned route is materially faster than requesting them ad hoc.

### Seasonally used homes

A system that stands unused for months needs sanitisation and flushing before use. Scheduling that before arrival is better than discovering it on arrival.

## How it works

### 01 — Survey the systems

What is fitted, what condition it is in, and what the realistic service interval is for this property's water and usage. A contract written from assumptions is a contract that disappoints.

### 02 — Choose a tier

Visit count and included consumables, matched to the systems and the duty. Tiers exist because a single under-sink unit and a villa with a softener are not the same commitment.

### 03 — Scheduled visits

We contact you when each is due, at a time you choose. Consumables in your tier are carried to the visit rather than ordered after it.

### 04 — Records and reporting

What was serviced, what was replaced, the readings taken, and the next due date. Commercial contracts include the water-quality log the premises needs.

## Benefits

### The remembering is ours

This is the actual product. Everything else in a contract is available piecemeal; the reminder is not.

### Predictable cost

One annual figure rather than an unpredictable sequence of call-outs, cartridges and labour charges.

### The expensive parts last longer

On-time pre-filter changes are what protect membranes and softener resin. Contracts exist largely to make that happen reliably.

### Faults are found at a visit, not at a failure

Tank charges, valve conditions and pressure drift are all checked routinely, which is when they are cheap to correct.

### Transferable within the UAE

A contract follows you to a new property rather than being written off when you move.

## Maintenance

### Visit frequency follows duty

Two visits a year suits a single under-sink unit in a small household. A villa with several treatment points, or a commercial kitchen, needs more.

### Consumables list is the thing to read

The difference between tiers is what is included. A membrane or a UV lamp included in the tier is worth more than an extra visit that carries neither.

### Records travel with the property

A serviced system with history is straightforward to maintain. One without is an investigation each time.

## Common problems

### Contract bought that does not cover the expensive consumable

- **Cause**: Not reading which consumables the tier includes.
- **Fix**: Match the tier to the systems you actually have. A UV lamp or a membrane excluded from the tier is the item most likely to surprise you.

### Visits scheduled but nobody home

- **Cause**: Fixed dates rather than agreed ones.
- **Fix**: Visits are confirmed with you rather than assigned. A missed visit is a lapsed schedule, which defeats the point.

### Contract on a system already beyond repair

- **Cause**: Signing before surveying.
- **Fix**: The survey comes first. Putting a maintenance contract on a system that needs replacing is not a service we want to sell.

## Choosing

### Count the treatment points, not the properties

One villa can be three systems. That is what sets the sensible visit count.

### Check what is excluded

The exclusions tell you more than the inclusions. Membranes, UV lamps and softener resin are the items worth confirming.

### Match the interval to your water

A tanker-fed villa genuinely needs more frequent pre-filter attention than a mains-fed apartment. A contract that ignores that is the wrong contract.

### Ask what the records look like

For commercial premises this is not optional. For domestic it is what makes the next service efficient.

## UAE context

Maintenance contracts do more work in the UAE than in a temperate, mains-fed market, for the same two reasons that shorten cartridge life here: sediment loading from tanks and tanker supply, and heat accelerating biological growth in housings and standing water.

Geography matters too. Outside Dubai and Abu Dhabi, attendance is on scheduled routes rather than same-day. A contract puts your visits inside those routes, which turns 'within 24 to 48 hours' from a constraint into a plan.
